Weather Prediction Markets Are Booming. Can They Actually Improve Forecasts?

Weather prediction markets are rapidly growing as traders wager on hurricanes, temperatures, and other meteorological events. If these markets beat traditional forecasts, they prove crowd intelligence outperforms expert analysis in another critical domain.

What You Need to Know
  • Traders are already pricing hurricane paths better than the National Weather Service in some scenarios.
  • Study weather prediction markets because they reveal what professionals actually believe versus what they publicly forecast.
  • Watch temperature volatility markets, not precipitation ones, where information asymmetry still favors serious meteorological research over crowd guesses.
The Prob's Take

Weather markets will crush traditional forecasts on tail events, but don't expect them to beat the National Weather Service on your Tuesday afternoon. Arbitrage the gap between what meteorologists say and what the crowd will pay.

Kalshi Now Controls 89% of the U.S. Prediction Market as Regulated Trading Takes Over

Kalshi now controls 89% of U.S. prediction markets, crushing offshore rivals like Polymarket by being the only regulated platform Americans can legally use. This regulatory monopoly gives one company enormous pricing power over how Americans bet on elections, economics, and everything else.

What You Need to Know
  • Capture 89% market share and you don't need to compete on fees anymore, just compliance.
  • Kalshi's regulatory moat means it can charge what it wants while offshore platforms slowly fade.
  • Americans now funnel billions through one CFTC-approved exchange, turning prediction markets into infrastructure.
The Prob's Take

Kalshi's 89% grip means one platform now sets prices for US event derivatives. Sharp traders should assume worse odds and wider spreads, making offshore markets suddenly worth the friction again.

The Prob Notes 89% market share. One regulated player. That is not a market, that is a monopoly in formation.

via Bloomberg
How Prediction Markets Turned the World Into a Casino

Prediction markets have exploded beyond politics into celebrity deaths and weather, flooding the ecosystem with casual gamblers who don't know what they're doing. For serious bettors, this means more liquidity and more mispriced outcomes to exploit before the market corrects.

What You Need to Know
  • Liquidity exploded in fringe markets, turning celebrity gossip and weather into legitimate trading instruments.
  • Casual money floods non-political markets, creating systematic mispricings that reward anyone who actually knows odds.
  • Every new tradeable outcome adds surface area for sharp bettors to exploit retail confusion profitably.
The Prob's Take

Expansion breeds inefficiency. The casuals flooding these markets are creating edges everywhere, but you need to spot which ones have actual informational advantages versus which ones are just noise traps.
