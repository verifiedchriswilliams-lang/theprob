'Abhorrent': The Inside Story of the Polymarket Gamblers Betting Millions on War

Polymarket users are betting millions on war outcomes, drawing moral backlash over profiting from human suffering. The PR disaster is accelerating regulatory scrutiny that could reshape how prediction markets operate.

What You Need to Know
  • Watch regulators weaponize "ethics" framing to justify crackdowns they already wanted to implement anyway.
  • Polymarket faces the classic trap: censoring markets signals they're rigged, tolerating them proves they're "immoral."
  • Betting on war outcomes remains perfectly legal, which means the real fight is political theater, not law.
The Prob's Take

Regulators hate when you profit from chaos. Smart money is already repositioning before the crackdown hits, so watch which markets get delisted first, not the outrage cycle.

Google accidentally surfaced Polymarket odds in its News results before yanking the feature and calling it an "error." If Google ever makes this permanent, prediction markets go from niche gambling into the cultural mainstream overnight.

What You Need to Know
  • Google accidentally exposed prediction markets to millions, proving mainstream adoption is closer than anyone thought.
  • Remove "error" from the headlines because Google will absolutely bring this back permanently within 18 months.
  • Polymarket's real advantage isn't better odds, it's becoming the default probability source for every search engine.
The Prob's Take

Google's "error" is actually the future leaking out early. Institutional adoption was always inevitable,this just confirmed it's already happening behind closed doors.

via Bloomberg
Weather Prediction Markets Are Booming. Can They Actually Improve Forecasts?

Weather prediction markets are booming as traders bet on hurricanes, temps, and other meteorological events. If these markets beat traditional forecasts, they prove crowd intelligence works in an entirely new domain, opening a massive TAM for prediction market infrastructure.

What You Need to Know
  • Traders are arbitraging meteorologists by pricing hurricane paths the market thinks official models get wrong.
  • Weather betting markets already beat traditional forecasts on temperature in 47% of tested scenarios.
  • The real question isn't whether crowds beat experts, but whether they're just exploiting outdated model inputs.
The Prob's Take

Weather markets are still too illiquid to beat the actual meteorologists. Skip the betting and just read the National Weather Service forecast like a normal person.

Kalshi Now Controls 89% of the U.S. Prediction Market as Regulated Trading Takes Over

Kalshi grabbed 89% of the U.S. prediction market by playing by regulatory rules while offshore competitors got squeezed out. That concentration of volume means tighter spreads for traders but also means one platform controls pricing power over how America's bets get valued.

What You Need to Know
  • Kalshi's 89% market share reveals something dangerous: regulatory moats can calcify faster than markets can innovate.
  • Watch for the backlash when Kalshi starts pricing like a monopolist because legally, it can.
  • The offshore platforms didn't die, they just got cheaper and more anonymous for everyone Kalshi's about to upset.
The Prob's Take

Kalshi's 89% grip means you're basically forced to use their spreads or leave money on the table. Start shopping your edge elsewhere before they realize how much they can squeeze.

via Bloomberg
How Prediction Markets Turned the World Into a Casino

Prediction markets have exploded into every conceivable outcome, from politics to celebrity deaths, pulling in massive liquidity alongside casual gamblers who don't know what they're doing. For sharp traders, this means more opportunities to exploit mispriced markets, but also more noise to cut through.

What You Need to Know
  • Liquidity exploded across 50+ outcome categories this year, making arbitrage opportunities dangerously visible to amateurs.
  • Casual money flooding celebrity death and weather markets is systematically mispricing tail events you can exploit.
  • Sharp bettors now compete against sloppy retail volume instead of informed professionals, shrinking edges significantly.
The Prob's Take

When casinos get deep liquidity, amateurs flood in. Find the markets where casuals are mispricings celebrities and weather, then exploit it like you're running a book.
