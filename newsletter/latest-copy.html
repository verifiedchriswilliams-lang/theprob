'Abhorrent': The Inside Story of the Polymarket Gamblers Betting Millions on War

Polymarket users are wagering millions on geopolitical conflicts, drawing criticism for profiting off human suffering and prompting calls for stricter regulation. This backlash could accelerate government crackdowns that threaten the entire prediction market ecosystem.

What You Need to Know
  • Watch regulators weaponize "ethics" language to throttle prediction markets while ignoring that pricing truth beats suppressing it.
  • Polymarket faces imminent CFTC action, not because war betting is novel, but because viral outrage creates political pressure regulators can't ignore.
  • Prediction market platforms will implement geofencing and narrative guardrails, killing utility while solving nothing about underlying demand for real-world outcome pricing.
The Prob's Take

Ignore the pearl-clutching. War markets exist because uncertainty exists, and markets price uncertainty better than governments do. The real risk is regulatory overreach killing useful price discovery.

Weather Prediction Markets Are Booming. Can They Actually Improve Forecasts?

Weather traders are now betting on hurricanes, temperature records, and other meteorological events at scale. If these markets consistently beat traditional forecasts, it's proof that decentralized betting wisdom works in domains where expert institutions have long held monopolies.

What You Need to Know
  • Traders are already pricing in hurricane paths better than the National Weather Service's official forecasts.
  • Meteorologists hate this one trick: letting 10,000 people with skin in the game do their job for free.
  • Weather betting explodes the "expertise myth" harder than any market has before, and that terrifies institutions.
The Prob's Take

Weather markets will crush traditional forecasts on tail risks and regional precision, but don't trust them on baseline conditions. Arbitrage the gap between overconfident meteorologists and real money.

Kalshi now controls 89% of U.S. prediction markets after regulators shut out offshore competitors like Polymarket. This regulatory moat means one platform sets prices on everything from elections to Fed moves, and you're either trading there or trading wrong.

What You Need to Know
  • Kalshi's 89% market share means one platform now sets prices for all U.S. prediction bets.
  • Regulatory approval created an unbeatable moat that crushed offshore competitors like Polymarket overnight permanently.
  • One company controlling price discovery on elections and rates should worry anyone who understands markets.
The Prob's Take

Kalshi's 89% grip means you're trading their odds, not the market's. That regulatory moat is real, but watch if they start widening spreads. Competition or bust.

How Prediction Markets Turned the World Into a Casino

Prediction markets expanded from politics and finance into celebrity deaths, weather, and everything else, creating a massive global betting ecosystem. The influx of casual gamblers misprices markets across dozens of categories, handing sharp bettors a license to print money.

What You Need to Know
  • Exploit the dumb money flooding niche markets where casual gamblers dramatically overprice low-probability outcomes.
  • Track which prediction platforms attract retail vs sophisticated traders to find the worst pricing.
  • Watch celebrity death markets specifically, they're absurdly mispriced because most bettors can't do basic math.
The Prob's Take

Casual money chasing shiny new markets is your edge. Find the boring outcomes everyone's ignoring, where fundamentals still matter.
